# OneDayBooks — Unicorn Path

## Strategic identity
OneDayBooks is not optimized to remain a publishing agency.
The target company is an **AI-native Publishing OS** that can become publishing infrastructure for authors, publishers, companies and institutions.

## Core transformation
Service → Productized Service → AI Publishing OS → Platform → Publishing Infrastructure

## North Star
Paid publishing customers today.
Scalable publishing throughput per unit of founder/human labor tomorrow.

## Key constraint
Revenue must grow faster than founder labor.

## Product thesis
A manuscript should move through a structured path:
intake → diagnosis → editing → proofreading → design → metadata/ISBN → print/ebook prep → distribution → launch → analytics.

AI performs repeatable work.
Humans approve exceptions, rights-sensitive decisions, brand-sensitive design and final publication decisions.

## Business model evolution
### Stage 1
19/49/300만원 service packages.

### Stage 2
Productized workflows and standardized deliverables.

### Stage 3
Subscription / workflow access / project fee.

### Stage 4
Transaction revenue from print, distribution, vendor marketplace, promotion and related publishing services.

### Stage 5
B2B/B2B2C publishing infrastructure for organizations and publishers.

## Moats to build
- workflow data
- publishing completion data
- exception handling knowledge
- templates and reusable production assets
- vendor/distribution integrations
- trust and case studies
- creator/community distribution

## Kill conditions
Do not confuse a busy service business with a scalable platform.
If revenue increases only when founder hours increase, remain disciplined: automate, standardize or remove the step.

## Weekly execution order
1. Close revenue
2. Instrument the funnel
3. Document the workflow
4. Automate the repeated work
5. Measure founder time
6. Improve conversion
7. Build referral/repeat loop
8. Only then scale paid acquisition

## Portfolio connection
- KeywordTrip supplies a reusable search→answer→action growth capability.
- Qsapiens supplies retention/community capability.
- K-Jam supplies marketplace/network-effect learning.
- OneDayBooks is the principal candidate to convert these capabilities into a defensible AI-native business.

## Current strategic decision
No new standalone business should outrank OneDayBooks unless it demonstrates stronger evidence in revenue, repeatability, CAC/LTV, retention, or network effects.
